Capo Chord Translator

Type chord names. Get the barre shape + frets from the capo, using your rules.

Preview with problems highlighted
PWC(shape/fret): shape must be F, F# or B. PWC tokens are passed through and checked, since no conversion rule was given for them.
These four shapes plus PWC are the only chords in the system; a custom chord still follows one of them. Strings 6 to 1 run left to right. Dots show the finger numbers. The number after the shape (F3, F7...) is the fret the bar sits on. When it is 0 (+0, so the capo/nut is the bar) the finger-1 bar is removed and you play only the remaining notes.
